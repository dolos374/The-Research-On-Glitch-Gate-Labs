Research on Glitch Gate Labs: a small studio with ambitions built to scale.

Glitch Gate Labs is developing a portfolio that reaches beyond a single game: entertainment, software, digital communities and platforms designed to compound influence over time.

Research and reporting by Johnaton Dolos

Editorial thesis

Power in technology is rarely created by one launch. It is built through products, distribution, communities, partnerships and the capacity to keep shipping.

Front Page

Product intelligence / Entertainment

Strike Tap Out — promotional artwork supplied for this publication. Image optimized for web delivery.
01 / Strike Tap Out

The first signal is already on the field.

Strike Tap Out represents Glitch Gate Labs' push into interactive entertainment: a fast, visually assertive baseball experience designed for immediate play and broad digital distribution.

For this research on Glitch Gate Labs, the significance is not only the game. It is the capability demonstrated by building, packaging and preparing a product for distribution.

For an emerging studio, every release can become infrastructure for the next one—an audience, a production pipeline, a recognizable identity and evidence that the organization can move from concept to market.

Why it matters

Strategic reading

01 — DISTRIBUTION

Build once. Reach many.

Web-first products can travel quickly across platforms, reducing friction between development, discovery and audience growth.

02 — PORTFOLIO

One product is a beachhead.

Games can establish creative and technical capabilities that later support apps, content, tools and larger digital ventures.

03 — IDENTITY

Brand becomes an asset.

A consistent visual and product language makes future launches easier to recognize and gives an expanding portfolio a common center of gravity.

04 — OPTIONALITY

The strategic advantage is what comes next.

The strongest early-stage position is not certainty. It is having enough capability and momentum to pursue several valuable directions.

Second Front

Networks / Markets / Community

United Tradira — supplied brand artwork.
Emerging platform

United Tradira aims at the network around the market.

Positioned as a place for networking, connecting and practicing trading, United Tradira points Glitch Gate Labs toward a different category of digital product: one where the community itself can become part of the experience.

The strategic idea is straightforward but consequential. Instead of treating a platform as a static destination, build a space where people return to learn, meet, practice and develop relationships.

From entertainment to networks: the portfolio begins to suggest a broader ambition.
